1

Speed

PDF ↓

Tests: throughput_ot3d, orszag_tang_3d

(a) Cell updates per second on one H100, on 3D Orszag–Tang run for a short time, 64³ to 512³, the fastest of the repeats (some of the rerun's repeats were slowed 3 to 12 times by something else on the node; the originals' repeats agree to 0.1 %); filled symbols are grids divisible by 6, where the Float16 kernels use their larger tile. (b) Time to t = 1 of 3D Orszag–Tang at 512³: one H100 for Abel et al.'s kernels and AthenaK, 128 CPU cores for RAMSES and Athena++.

Read it: Abel et al.'s kernels ran at Courant number 0.25 in their own rule (the sum over the three directions), the other codes at 0.3 against the fastest single direction, so the kernels took about 3.3 times more steps; the times in (b) carry that handicap.

Accuracy for the GPU time spent

PDF ↓

Tests: entropy, alfven_oblique, field_loop

Since the full rerun (2026-10-21) the fixed kernels of the sweep-fixes branch are drawn in full colour and the originals they fix in a pale tint, marked “original”. Error at the end against the exact answer, against the GPU time the run took, for the codes that ran on the H100. Each point is one grid, 32² to 512². A run that took longer than the same test on twice the grid is left out: its time is not the solver's (three 32² runs of the fixed CT prototype, where compiling leaked into the timing).

Read it: Lower and further left is better. A curve that turns up stops converging: the original half-precision kernels. Fixed, they converge.

Accuracy on every planar test

Tests: entropy, alfven, alfven_oblique, field_loop_novz, brio_wu, brio_wu_rotated, orszag_tang, rotor, blast_2d_b0, blast_2d_b50, blast_2d_b100, kh_uniform, current_sheet, gem

Relative L1 error at the end of each test (density, or the field where the density does not change, and for GEM), divided by the median of the same-order CT set-ups: AthenaK PLM/HLLE, RAMSES HLL and Athena++ PLM/HLLE. Against the exact answer where there is one, otherwise against a time-matched AthenaK run at 4096² averaged to the grid. Faint symbols are the external codes' best set-ups and AREPO, for context. Since the full rerun (2026-10-21) the fixed kernels of the sweep-fixes branch are drawn in full colour and the originals they fix in a pale tint, marked “original”.

Read it: 1 means as accurate as the same-order CT codes; the shaded band is ±25 %. Kelvin–Helmholtz at t = 3 is turbulent, so its error also measures where the vortices happen to be.

Order of convergence

PDF ↓

Tests: entropy, alfven, alfven_oblique

L1 error against grid size on the three smooth waves with exact answers. Top row: Abel et al.'s kernels and the CT prototype; bottom row: the external codes. Dotted and dashed lines: first and second order. Since the full rerun (2026-10-21) the fixed kernels of the sweep-fixes branch are drawn in full colour and the originals they fix in a pale tint, marked “original”.

Read it: Second-order schemes follow the dashed slope. The original half-precision kernels level off or rise past 64 to 128 cells and the original CT prototype follows the first-order slope; every fixed kernel follows the second-order slope to 512².

Robustness

PDF ↓

Tests: every test

Every test, grid and set-up of the sweep: a full square is a run that reached the end time; a partly filled one shows how far a run got before its own solver stopped it. The fixed kernels take the columns of the originals they fix; the originals' counts are under the key. A run whose magnetic or kinetic energy ends above 10 times AthenaK PLM/HLLE's on the same grid counts as failed from the time it got there: the fixed PPM kernel at 512² on the field loop and the confined-field Kelvin–Helmholtz test (field energy 92 and 156 times AthenaK's at the end; the original stopped on both). No other run of the original or fixed kernels trips this.

Read it: Columns are set-ups, rows are tests, and the five squares of a cell are the grids 32 to 512. The counts at the bottom are the runs that finished.

6

Leakage into a field-free region

PDF ↓

Tests: kh_localized, kh_localized_dtjump

(a) Field strength at t = 3 on 512², with the field-free vortex circled; the last two maps are fixed kernels. (b) The field inside that vortex over the reference's, against grid size. (c) The largest cleaning potential ψ through a sudden 40-fold cut of the timestep, at 256². Since the full rerun (2026-10-21) the fixed kernels of the sweep-fixes branch are drawn in full colour and the originals they fix in a pale tint, marked “original”.

Read it: In (b) the CT codes sit at 1, and so does the fixed CT prototype (0.9 to 1.7; the original was 1600 times at 64²). The GLM kernels sit 7.5 to 13 times above at 512² and level off rather than converge; the Float16 kernel with cell-centred CT in place of GLM sits at 5.7 to 6.3 at every grid. In (c) ψ jumps with the cleaning speed, which follows the timestep, except in the constant-c_h variant.

7

Two tests of the divergence constraint

PDF ↓

Tests: brio_wu_rotated, field_loop

(a) Rotated shock tube at 256²: the largest error in the field normal to the shock, which must stay exactly 0.75. (b) Field loop with motion out of the plane at 128²: the rms of the field B_z that grows only from divergence errors.

Read it: Further left is better. Constrained-transport codes keep both at round-off; cleaning keeps them small but finite. The Float16 kernel with cell-centred CT keeps the normal field to 5×10⁻⁵ but lets B_z grow to 4×10⁻² of B₀, six times the fixed f16/HLL kernel; the CT prototype, original and fixed, keeps the normal field near 10⁻⁶ but its B_z is 1 to 4×10⁻³, not round-off.

8

Advecting a field loop

PDF ↓

Tests: field_loop_novz, field_loop_3d

Magnetic energy of the advected loop over its starting value: (a) in the plane at 64², (b) the inclined loop in 3D at 128³, with Gardiner & Stone's fitted decay at those grids.

Read it: The exact answer keeps the energy constant; the decay is numerical diffusion. The fixed CT prototype loses energy like the other second-order codes; the original lost two thirds by t = 2 in the plane.

9

Supersonic decaying turbulence

PDF ↓

Tests: decay_supersonic

Mach 9 decay with a mean field at 512³ to t = 0.2: (a) kinetic and (b) magnetic energy against time; (c) velocity and (d) magnetic spectra at the end.

Read it: The codes agree on the energies; at high wavenumber the spectra show each scheme's dissipation. No fixed kernel reached the end at 512³ (seed 1): the fixed CT prototype stops at Mach 9, the Float16 kernels stop as their originals did.
