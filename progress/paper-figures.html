Tomida et al. (2026)

arXiv:2605.07928 ↗

Constrained transport against mixed divergence cleaning. Kelvin–Helmholtz instability with a weak field: uniform, confined to one layer, and with a sudden timestep change. The paper's CT, D1v/Dhv (cleaning speed following the timestep) and D1c (constant speed) correspond to our CT prototype and AthenaK/RAMSES/Athena++, the four GLM kernels with c_h ∝ 1/Δt, and the constant-c_h kernel.
