Every test, every grid, every code

26 tests from 32 to 512 cells a side, run with the five GLM kernels and the CT prototype of T. Abel, AthenaK, RAMSES and Athena++, each in a setup matched to the GPU kernels and in its best setup, against exact solutions or AthenaK references at 4096² and 512³. It reproduces the comparisons of Tomida et al. (2026), Kritsuk et al. (2011), Pakmor et al. (2026), the GEM challenge and Gardiner & Stone (2005, 2008), then extends each to all codes and grids.

Assumptions

  • Step counts per grid are Sherlock medians from the completed campaign; throughputs are measured H100 medians by grid.
  • CPU rates: RAMSES 3D measured on Sherlock (0.08–0.09 M cell-steps/s per core); RAMSES 2D and Athena++ timed on this Mac and scaled by RAMSES's Sherlock/Mac ratio. The Athena++ figures are the least certain (the range is 0.5–1.5×).
  • AthenaK PPM/HLLD/RK3 stalls at Mach 9 (Δt collapse); it runs only at 64³ and 128³ there. Every run has a step cap of 4× its expected steps. The sweep found that RAMSES matched (HLL) stalls there too: at 64³ and 128³ it reaches 10–20% of the run before the cap, so its 256³ and 512³ runs would cost far more than planned to record the same stall.
  • Returned data counts files up to 50 MB (larger 3D states stay on scratch, listed per run); each run also has a small summary archive.
