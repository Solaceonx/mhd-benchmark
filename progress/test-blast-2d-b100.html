06 · Power spectra

All codes on shared axes · N=128, t=0.01, seed 42

Left: normalized variance per logarithmic interval of k, k E(k) divided by the total, where E(k) is the shell-summed power (round(|k|) bins, mean excluded); k E(k) is 4πk³P(k) in 3D and 2πk²P(k) in 2D, and each curve has area 1 over ln k, so the plot shows where the variance sits. Right: ratio of the unnormalized power to the reference. For 3D, recorded full-volume spectra are used; 2D spectra come from actual plane states. Small reference power is excluded from ratios. AthenaK 4096² → 128 (restricted spectrum). Late chaotic flows need statistical interpretation.
