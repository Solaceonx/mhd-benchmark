Why the Mach 9 runs failed

Of 88 attempted method/grid/seed combinations, 43 reached t = 0.2 with finite saved diagnostics, 37 stopped early, and 8 reported completion while their output became nonfinite. Another 4 were explicitly skipped. These counts use repeat 1, rather than counting repeated timing runs as independent experiments.

This is decaying MHD turbulence: initial Mach 9, γ = 1.01, mean Bz = 0.3, two seeds, 64³–512³. Strong shocks and rarefactions test pressure/density positivity; magnetic amplification and cleaning add further numerical constraints. The Mach 30 and Mach 50 driven runs use zero magnetic field, different kernels/settings and γ − 1 = 10⁻⁶. A larger Mach number alone does not make them a stricter version of this test.

Half precision has a largest finite value of 65,504. CPU checks reproduce flux overflow and show why ch ∝ 1/Δt can worsen it on steps shortened for output. They also identify the HLLD tolerance issue. The 3D records usually report the downstream invalid-state check, so those mechanisms should not be presented as a traced cause of every individual stop.
