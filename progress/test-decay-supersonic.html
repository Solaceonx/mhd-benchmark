Set-up

Supersonic decaying MHD turbulence inspired by Kritsuk et al. (2011): rms Mach 9, near-isothermal gamma = 1.01, mean field B0 = 0.3 along z (beta0 = 22). Starts from a smooth random field, not their pre-driven saturated state.

After: after Kritsuk et al. (2011). The same initial state goes to every code.
