Density statistics require care

Up to 0.35% of cells lie at the density floor in a saved observation, and RAMSES has up to 1,363 nonpositive-density cells. The density distribution is affected by numerical floors, so its width is not a reliable physical measurement here. Completion alone does not establish physical validity.

How this differs from Mach 9 decay

The Mach 9 problem is decaying MHD with a mean magnetic field and γ = 1.01. These Mach 30 runs are driven hydro with B = 0 and γ − 1 = 10⁻⁶. Abel et al.'s runs here use Float32 shared storage; the failing half-precision production kernels from the Mach 9 sweep are not these configurations. The two experiments test different numerical regimes.
