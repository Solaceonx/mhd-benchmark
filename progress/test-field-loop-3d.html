01 · Error norms versus physical time

All codes on shared axes · N=128, seed 42

Relative L1, L2, and Linf use the matching reference norm; zero-field blasts use absolute magnetic error. Magnetic error is a vector difference. Markers are outputs, not a complete history when sparse. Symlog retains zero with a linear band at least 10^-5 of the panel peak, so roundoff does not stretch the scale. Reference: kinematic periodic-return target; weak Lorentz forces make this approximate.

04 · Conservation and energy evolution

All codes on shared axes · N=128, seed 42

These sweep tests are periodic and unforced. Component energies may change physically; mass and total-energy drift reveal budget errors. Momentum uses initial mass × initial sound speed, so zero initial momentum is safe. Curves stop at the first nonfinite output.

06 · Power spectra

All codes on shared axes · N=128, t=1, seed 42

Left: normalized variance per logarithmic interval of k, k E(k) divided by the total, where E(k) is the shell-summed power (round(|k|) bins, mean excluded); k E(k) is 4πk³P(k) in 3D and 2πk²P(k) in 2D, and each curve has area 1 over ln k, so the plot shows where the variance sits. Right: ratio of the unnormalized power to the reference. For 3D, recorded full-volume spectra are used; 2D spectra come from actual plane states. Small reference power is excluded from ratios. No independent reference spectrum at the selected time. Late chaotic flows need statistical interpretation.
