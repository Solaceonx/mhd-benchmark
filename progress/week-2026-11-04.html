  • Field transport: gPLUTO parabolic/HLLD/RK3, GLM (1.8×), AthenaK PPM/HLLD/RK3 (2.2×), AthenaPK PPM/HLLD/RK3, GLM (2.2×)

Speed per cell is not speed to an answer. Abel et al. GLM tiled LLF (original path) makes the most cell updates per second, 8.8× as many as Cholla PPMC/HLLD/VL, CT, yet Cholla PPMC/HLLD/VL, CT reaches the accuracy in the least time (median 1.2× the fastest, against 10×). A more diffusive solver needs a finer grid, and in 2D the work grows as the grid's cube (cells × steps). Orszag–Tang:

04Convergence without a truth

Each set-up against its own finest grid, the differences between successive grids, and Richardson extrapolation, set beside its error against the true solution, for the 13 planar tests (scripts/self_convergence.py). Medians over the set-ups:

testsown finest ÷ true errororder from differencesorder against truthRichardson estimate ÷ true errorRichardson gain
Smooth waves0.852.02.01.11.1
Shocks0.610.70.81.90.6
Kelvin–Helmholtz, current sheet0.521.20.10.430.98
  • Against its own finest grid a set-up looks better than it is: its error seems 15 % smaller on smooth waves, 39 % with shocks, 48 % on Kelvin–Helmholtz and the current sheet.
  • Grid differences give the true order on smooth waves. On Kelvin–Helmholtz and the current sheet the differences shrink at order 1.2 while the error against the truth barely falls (order 0.1): the grids agree with each other, not with the truth.
  • Richardson extrapolation estimates the error well on smooth waves, but does not bring the answer nearer the truth: the limiters change the error's pattern from grid to grid.
  • An own-grid reference hides a code that converges to the wrong answer: GIZMO's Alfvén-wave error stops falling at about 0.1 %, and AREPO's static mesh on the field loop agrees with itself while its field blows up.

05Getting the data right

An audit of our own records and conversions before the final rebuild.

  • The new codes' records held srun's wall time, start-up included (gPLUTO Brio–Wu 32²: 456 s, of which 18 s computing): every record now has the code's own clock.
  • Speeds are taken from each set-up's largest 3D run: a planar run leaves a GPU mostly idle, and Abel et al.'s kernels run the planar tests on N³ cells, so planar speeds favoured them.
  • Our GIZMO conversion made the field on the grid 0.05 % too strong (it divided by the cell volume, but GIZMO's particle volumes add up to 1.0005 of the box). Fixed, and all 81 complete runs reconverted from GIZMO's snapshots: its Alfvén-wave error at 512² went from 1.16×10⁻³ to 1.02×10⁻³; the floor that is left is GIZMO's own.
  • A stop on the second of two GPUs was read as a lost job (gPLUTO, Cholla): now the code's own stop and reason.
  • AREPO's static mesh on the field loop finishes, but its field error is 102× the field at 64² and 373× at 128²: flagged, not yet marked invalid.
